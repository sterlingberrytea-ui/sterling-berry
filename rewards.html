Common Questions

Rewards FAQ

Do my points expire? ▾
Points do not expire as long as your account has had at least one purchase or activity in the past 12 months. Inactive accounts (no purchases or activity for 12 months) may have their points reset.
How do I redeem points at checkout? ▾
At checkout, you'll see a "Redeem Points" option. Enter the number of points you'd like to apply and the equivalent discount will be subtracted from your order total automatically.
Can I earn points on gift card purchases? ▾
Points are earned on gift card purchases, but not when a gift card is redeemed. So if you buy a $50 gift card, you earn 50+ points. The recipient does not earn points when they use the card.
How quickly are points added after a purchase? ▾
Points are added to your account when your order ships — typically within 1–2 business days of placing your order. Review and referral points are added manually and may take up to 5 business days.
What happens if I return an order? ▾
Points earned on returned orders will be deducted from your balance when the return is processed. If this brings your balance below a tier threshold, you may move to a lower tier.
Can I combine points with other discounts or promo codes? ▾
Points can be combined with most promotions. However, they cannot be stacked with new-customer discount codes or other active promo codes that specify "no combining with other offers."
