About Us

Why We Started Sterling Berry

Sterling Berry began as a simple idea: what if your everyday cup of tea could be as carefully crafted as a fine espresso? Our family set out to change how people experience tea in Raleigh, NC.

We are famous for our signature North Carolina fig leaf infusions — a unique blend that captures the earthy, subtly-sweet essence of locally harvested fig leaves.
